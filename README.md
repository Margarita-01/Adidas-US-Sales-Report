# Adidas-US-Sales-Report
## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyze Adidas US sales performance between 2020 and 2021. The analysis explores overall sales and profitability while comparing performance across products, retailers, sales methods, regions, and cities. Interactive filters allow users to explore performance by month and year.

## 🎯Key Insights & KPIs
●Total Sales: $899.90M

●Total Operating Profit: $332.13M

●Profit Margin: 36.9%

●Total Units Sold: 2M

●In-store sales generated the highest operating profit, followed by outlet and online sales.

●Men’s Street Footwear recorded the highest sales among the products displayed.

●The West region recorded the strongest overall sales and operating profit.

●Several cities, including Chester and New York, recorded particularly high sales.

●Sales performance varies across retailers, highlighting differences in contribution by retail channel.

## Recommendations
●Prioritize high-performing products such as Men’s Street Footwear while monitoring other product categories for growth opportunities.

●Strengthen performance in lower-performing regions through targeted sales and marketing strategies.

●Continue supporting the in-store sales channel while exploring ways to increase online sales.

●Identify the factors driving strong performance in top cities and apply similar strategies to lower-performing locations.

●Monitor retailer and regional performance regularly to identify changes in sales and profitability.
